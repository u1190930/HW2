ONE AI SUGGESTION TESTED AND REVISED
An earlier validation implementation limited Card Value to two decimal places.
The requirements specify a positive number, and the schema uses FLOAT without
that precision limit. The validation was revised to accept any finite positive
numeric value. A value of 12.345 was tested and accepted; zero and negative
values were tested and rejected.
